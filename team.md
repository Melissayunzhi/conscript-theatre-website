    roles: Performer, Director, Dramaturg, Writer, Audio and Video Design, Production
    photo: https://static.wixstatic.com/media/8ca848_60013c5731dc491f8f34129d2ed7ebdf~mv2.jpg/v1/crop/x_1,y_0,w_518,h_705/fill/w_574,h_788,al_c,q_85/8ca848_60013c5731dc491f8f34129d2ed7ebdf~mv2.jpg

    bio: |
      Elia is a British-German theatre practitioner, writer, and interdisciplinary artist.

      Their philosophy of Hyper-materialism, and development of this as a methodology of Theatrical Practice, is the driving force behind Conscript's work. They're a trained physical and post-dramatic performer, and enjoy handstands. In their spare time, Elia makes experimental music, and writes fact and fiction about ecology, politics, science, and history.

      Elia has a strong internal sense of direction, and knows where North is about 80% of the time.

      They're also the only one who knows how all the old technical equipment we use works: "It doesn't", Elia says.
  - name: Guy Rudin
    title: ""

    photo: https://static.wixstatic.com/media/cde7b5_4d5c4bb19a4b4b98b073d82c1d225d05~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_602,h_814,al_c,q_85/cde7b5_4d5c4bb19a4b4b98b073d82c1d225d05~mv2.jpg

    bio: |
      Guy is a Bristol based Theatre Maker, Actor, and Plumber.

      Guy plays the trumpet and reads lots of books about history and sociology. At heart, Guy is an anthropologist - and he loves to explore this by making strange and poetic performances where he shouts, sings, leaps and trembles at poor and unsuspecting audiences. He is an experienced physical performer and deviser.

      Guy and Elia shared a flat when they were training in Estonia, and have both independently described the experience as "like living 40 years of marriage within the span of 3 months".

      They remain the closest of friends.

    title: ""
    roles: Performer, Movement Director, Writer
    photo: https://static.wixstatic.com/media/8ca848_9dcad64c6ad7404991cd5de2192d5cb7~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_574,h_714,al_c,q_85/8ca848_9dcad64c6ad7404991cd5de2192d5cb7~mv2.jpg

    bio: |
      Nastia is an interdisciplinary performance artist from Odesa, Ukraine. At the core of her practice is movement and body work, creating powerful symbolic images and engaging stories.

      She engages in collaborative processes using multimedia, poetic language, and postreferences to devise evocative yet vulnerable theatre.

      She likes to play movement against the qualities of the other materials on stage, to create dialogue of a completely pre-linguistic kind.

      She trained in acrobatics and can do a full aerial. It's very impressive. I've seen it.
  - name: Emile Belli
    title: ""
    roles: Performer, Musician, Sound Design, Writing, Direction
    photo: https://static.wixstatic.com/media/cde7b5_17bcb2a06dd64d7c9f5da818f2d6aa3b~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_590,h_700,al_c,q_85/cde7b5_17bcb2a06dd64d7c9f5da818f2d6aa3b~mv2.png
    photo_hover: ""
    bio: |
      Emile is a French-Italian multidisciplinary artist specialised in Sound Design and Mime.

      Central to his practice is the notion of listening as a creative tool - to sounds, to the body, to images - even listening to listening itself!

      He welcomes incongruencies and contradictions, and likes to present multiple realities simultaneously using the tools of dynamic foley, expressive physical performance, and creative sound design.

      He is unsure whether he likes things having meaning or not.

      He dreams one day to be a tape recorder.
