Surely Some Revelation is at Hand is a show about technology, bureaucracy, and sending a rocket to the sun because that’s where we think God will hear us.

Two performers dressed as scientists (or maybe Mormons) are in a room (on a stage) setting up equipment, watching videos, listening to tapes, singing songs, trying to mend a printer, having a crisis of faith, acting out the past, rerecording history, and arguing with each other.

This metaphysical smorgasbord of reality and fiction is presented via a tapestry of physical performance, poetic text, video installation, historical fiction, speculative sci-fi, live camerawork and noise music.

This is done through a miserably overcomplicated web of analogue A/V Equipment, which the performers desperately try to make work properly.
