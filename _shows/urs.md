  Untitled (Robot Story) has been in development since early 2026.

Untitled (Robot Story) is an experimental, multimedia piece of theatre about robotic military machinery, memory, and love.

A robot has woken up with more memories than it's supposed to have. A drone looks for too long at birds, kitchens, hilltops. A couple navigates love in times of war.

*But none of that is real, really.*

What happens when our technology catches up with our fiction?

How do we tell stories, when our tropes are now traps?

And what responsibilities do we actually have, as artists, when we make work about real things in the real world?

<small>Untitled (Robot Story) is told through experimental sound and video design, physical theatre, projection, live A/V composition, puppetry, dance, 3D Modelling and Animation, live camerawork, poetic text, and -sometimes- acting.</small>
